Personality LMS Research Demo

A content foundation for a SvelteKit demo where Aephoria-style Enneagram results connect main type, trifix, instincts, team dynamics, and LMS course recommendations.

Product Framing

The recommended model is Aephoria-style but pragmatic: show a main type, an ordered trifix, and optional instinct stack, then connect those signals to practical skill development.

This keeps the demo rich and visual without turning the first LMS build into a full subtype encyclopedia.

Recommended Result Model

Primary

Main Type

The highest weighted type becomes the user's main pattern and gets the strongest visual treatment in the results view.

Signature

Trifix

The top three scores become an ordered signature such as 3-8-5, showing the user's lead pattern and supporting strategies.

Context

Instinct Stack

Use sp, so, and sx as compact profile context and scenario flavor. Defer full 27-subtype course paths until later.

Aephoria Type Headings

Type 1 · Body

Meticulous Judge

Builds integrity, standards, and quality without perfectionism or criticism.

Type 2 · Heart

Supportive Cheerleader

Builds empathy and care while keeping needs and limits visible.

Type 3 · Heart

Ambitious Task Master

Builds goal focus and momentum without tying identity to performance.

Type 4 · Heart

Sensitive Maverick

Builds depth, authenticity, and creative translation into action.

Type 5 · Head

Expert Observer

Builds expertise and clarity while moving before certainty is perfect.

Type 6 · Head

Vigilant Questioner

Builds preparedness, trust calibration, and confidence under pressure.

Type 7 · Head

Positive Dreamer

Builds option generation, reframing, and completion discipline.

Type 8 · Body

Tough Protector

Builds courage, directness, protection, and calibrated power.

Type 9 · Body

Adaptive Diplomat

Builds mediation, inclusion, steadiness, and productive conflict.

Course Recommendations

T3
Achievement Without Overidentification

For Ambitious Task Master main types or teams that depend heavily on performance. Turns execution into sustainable, value-aligned momentum.

T8
Constructive Assertiveness

For Tough Protector patterns that need calibration, or users who need stronger boundaries and directness.

T9
Perspective Holding and Productive Conflict

For Adaptive Diplomat patterns, missing Type 9 capacity, or teams that need to surface tension clearly.

T6
Risk Intelligence and Trust Under Pressure

For teams that need premortems, contingency planning, and risk language that does not spiral into anxiety.
