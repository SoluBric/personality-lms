# Enneagram Type Development Map

## Type 1: Meticulous Judge

- Traditional labels: Reformer / Perfectionist
- Center: Body / Instinctive
- Core capacity: principled improvement, quality, integrity
- Healthy expression: improves systems without shaming people or self
- Overused expression: rigidity, criticism, chronic dissatisfaction
- Underused expression: tolerating poor standards or avoiding responsibility
- Blind spots: impatience, moral overcertainty, difficulty relaxing
- Growth practices: self-compassion, iterative improvement, values-based prioritization
- LMS links: Quality Judgment, Self-Compassion for High Standards, Feedback Without Fixing

## Type 2: Supportive Cheerleader

- Traditional labels: Helper / Giver
- Center: Heart / Feeling
- Core capacity: empathy, support, relational generosity
- Healthy expression: offers help with clear boundaries
- Overused expression: people-pleasing, rescue behavior, indirect need expression
- Underused expression: emotional distance or lack of relational initiative
- Blind spots: hidden expectations, resentment, over-involvement
- Growth practices: boundary setting, asking directly, differentiating care from control
- LMS links: Boundaried Empathy, Asking for What You Need, Support Without Rescue

## Type 3: Ambitious Task Master

- Traditional labels: Achiever / Performer
- Center: Heart / Feeling
- Core capacity: goal focus, execution, adaptive performance
- Healthy expression: creates momentum while staying connected to values
- Overused expression: image management, burnout, worth tied to results
- Underused expression: low drive, difficulty translating ideas into outcomes
- Blind spots: emotional bypassing, performative productivity, impatience with slower processing
- Growth practices: values clarification, sustainable pacing, honest status reflection
- LMS links: Achievement Without Overidentification, Goals That Keep Their Soul, Sustainable High Performance

## Type 4: Sensitive Maverick

- Traditional labels: Individualist / Creative
- Center: Heart / Feeling
- Core capacity: emotional depth, authenticity, creative meaning
- Healthy expression: brings emotional truth and originality without becoming self-absorbed
- Overused expression: comparison, mood identification, feeling misunderstood
- Underused expression: shallow emotional vocabulary or fear of distinctive expression
- Blind spots: idealization, withdrawal, amplifying what is missing
- Growth practices: emotional regulation, disciplined creative output, appreciation practice
- LMS links: Emotional Literacy, Creative Practice Under Constraints, From Longing to Action

## Type 5: Expert Observer

- Traditional labels: Investigator / Observer
- Center: Head / Thinking
- Core capacity: analysis, expertise, conceptual clarity
- Healthy expression: shares knowledge generously and acts before certainty is perfect
- Overused expression: detachment, hoarding time/energy, analysis paralysis
- Underused expression: acting without enough understanding or weak mental models
- Blind spots: isolation, low emotional participation, delayed decision-making
- Growth practices: embodied action, concise communication, collaborative inquiry
- LMS links: Analysis to Action, Communicating Complexity Simply, Collaborative Expertise

## Type 6: Vigilant Questioner

- Traditional labels: Loyalist / Skeptic
- Center: Head / Thinking
- Core capacity: preparedness, trust, risk awareness
- Healthy expression: anticipates problems while building shared confidence
- Overused expression: suspicion, anxiety loops, excessive checking
- Underused expression: naive optimism or poor contingency thinking
- Blind spots: outsourcing authority, worst-case fixation, testing loyalty
- Growth practices: trust calibration, decision confidence, premortems with closure
- LMS links: Risk Intelligence, Trust Under Pressure, Decisive Preparedness

## Type 7: Positive Dreamer

- Traditional labels: Enthusiast / Optimist
- Center: Head / Thinking
- Core capacity: possibility, reframing, experimentation
- Healthy expression: energizes options while staying present to constraints
- Overused expression: distraction, avoidance of pain, overcommitment
- Underused expression: pessimism, lack of creative options, low energy for exploration
- Blind spots: unfinished work, minimizing discomfort, novelty seeking
- Growth practices: focus, completion rituals, staying with hard emotions
- LMS links: Focus for Fast Minds, Productive Reframing, Finishing What You Start

## Type 8: Tough Protector

- Traditional labels: Challenger / Protector
- Center: Body / Instinctive
- Core capacity: courage, directness, protection, decisive leadership
- Healthy expression: uses power to protect, clarify, and mobilize
- Overused expression: domination, intensity, dismissing vulnerability
- Underused expression: conflict avoidance, weak boundaries, difficulty taking charge
- Blind spots: impact of force, impatience, distrust of softness
- Growth practices: calibrated assertiveness, vulnerability, power sharing
- LMS links: Constructive Assertiveness, Power With Calibration, Difficult Conversations With Directness

## Type 9: Adaptive Diplomat

- Traditional labels: Peacemaker / Mediator
- Center: Body / Instinctive
- Core capacity: mediation, steadiness, inclusion, perspective holding
- Healthy expression: creates shared ground without disappearing
- Overused expression: conflict avoidance, inertia, self-forgetting
- Underused expression: low patience, poor listening, forcing premature decisions
- Blind spots: passive resistance, unclear priorities, merging with others' agendas
- Growth practices: priority setting, conflict engagement, clear preference expression
- LMS links: Perspective Holding, Productive Conflict, Acting From Your Own Agenda
