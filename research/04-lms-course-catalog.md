# LMS Course Catalog

Each course is demo-ready and can later become a Svelte content card, course detail page, module sequence, or seeded database row.

## Type-Linked Courses

### T1-QUALITY: Quality Judgment Without Perfectionism

- Linked type: 1
- Capacity: integrity, standards, improvement
- Level: foundation
- Format: micro-course
- Outcomes: distinguish standards from preferences; identify a good-enough threshold; give precise non-shaming feedback
- Modules: Standards vs Preferences; The Good-Enough Line; Repairing Critical Feedback
- Practice: rewrite a recent criticism into a clear request

### T2-BOUNDARIES: Boundaried Empathy

- Linked type: 2
- Capacity: support, care, boundaries
- Level: foundation
- Format: reflection sprint
- Outcomes: notice rescue impulses; ask before helping; state a limit without withdrawing care
- Modules: Help vs Rescue; Direct Needs; Warm Boundaries
- Practice: create a two-sentence boundary script for a real recurring situation

### T3-VALUES: Achievement Without Overidentification

- Linked type: 3
- Capacity: execution, goals, authentic performance
- Level: applied
- Format: scenario lab
- Outcomes: separate identity from outcomes; define value-aligned success; pace work sustainably
- Modules: Scoreboards and Self-Worth; Values-Based Goals; Sustainable Pace
- Practice: convert one external metric into a balanced scorecard with health and relationship signals

### T4-EMOTIONAL-LITERACY: Emotional Literacy in Motion

- Linked type: 4
- Capacity: emotional depth, meaning, creative expression
- Level: foundation
- Format: micro-course
- Outcomes: name emotions precisely; separate feeling from story; turn emotional insight into action
- Modules: Emotion Vocabulary; Story vs Signal; Creative Translation
- Practice: complete a feeling-to-action worksheet after a strong emotional reaction

### T5-ACTION: From Analysis to Action

- Linked type: 5
- Capacity: analysis, expertise, action under uncertainty
- Level: applied
- Format: scenario lab
- Outcomes: decide with incomplete information; share early thinking; set research limits
- Modules: The 70 Percent Brief; Useful Uncertainty; Explaining the Model
- Practice: produce a one-page recommendation with assumptions and next evidence needed

### T6-RISK: Risk Intelligence and Trust Under Pressure

- Linked type: 6
- Capacity: preparedness, trust, contingency planning
- Level: applied
- Format: team workshop
- Outcomes: run premortems; separate risk from fear; close the loop after planning
- Modules: Risk Language; Premortem Design; Confidence After Caution
- Practice: run a 20-minute premortem and choose exactly three mitigations

### T7-FOCUS: Focus for Fast Minds

- Linked type: 7
- Capacity: possibility, focus, completion
- Level: foundation
- Format: micro-course
- Outcomes: reduce option overload; stay with discomfort; complete a commitment
- Modules: Option Debt; The Discomfort Window; Completion Rituals
- Practice: close or complete three open loops and reflect on avoided discomfort

### T8-ASSERTIVENESS: Constructive Assertiveness

- Linked type: 8
- Capacity: courage, directness, boundaries
- Level: foundation
- Format: scenario lab
- Outcomes: make direct requests; calibrate intensity; protect without dominating
- Modules: Clean Directness; Force vs Clarity; Repair After Impact
- Practice: record a difficult conversation script in three intensity levels

### T9-MEDIATION: Perspective Holding and Productive Conflict

- Linked type: 9
- Capacity: mediation, inclusion, conflict engagement
- Level: applied
- Format: team workshop
- Outcomes: surface disagreement; represent multiple views; state personal priorities
- Modules: Listening for Stakes; Naming the Tension; Preference Before Consensus
- Practice: facilitate a structured disagreement using "what matters to each side" prompts

## Cross-Type Pathways

### Body Center Pathway: Boundaries, Action, and Standards

- Types: 8, 9, 1
- Use when: user or team needs more grounded execution, clearer limits, or steadier conflict engagement
- Courses: T8-ASSERTIVENESS, T9-MEDIATION, T1-QUALITY

### Heart Center Pathway: Identity, Care, and Achievement

- Types: 2, 3, 4
- Use when: user or team needs better emotional honesty, recognition patterns, or value-aligned goals
- Courses: T2-BOUNDARIES, T3-VALUES, T4-EMOTIONAL-LITERACY

### Head Center Pathway: Risk, Clarity, and Possibility

- Types: 5, 6, 7
- Use when: user or team needs better thinking discipline, uncertainty handling, or creative option generation
- Courses: T5-ACTION, T6-RISK, T7-FOCUS

## Course Recommendation Copy Examples

- "Recommended because Type 3 is one of your strongest patterns; this course helps turn achievement into sustainable, values-led execution."
- "Recommended because Type 8 is currently underused; this course builds clean assertiveness and stronger boundaries."
- "Recommended for your team because Type 6 capacity is low across the group; this workshop improves risk language without creating anxiety loops."
