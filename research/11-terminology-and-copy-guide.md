# Terminology and Copy Guide

This guide keeps the demo language consistent. It can change as the app's voice develops.

## Core Terms

### Use

- Main type
- Trifix
- Instinct stack
- Capacity
- Pattern
- Development focus
- Recommended pathway
- Team dynamics
- Strength
- Growth edge
- Underused capacity

### Avoid

- Diagnosis
- Disorder
- Fixed identity
- Scientifically proven type
- Predicts your future
- Weakness as a character flaw
- Bad type or good type

## Result Copy Rules

### Main Type

Pattern:

> Your main type is [number] / [heading]. This suggests [capacity] is one of your most available patterns.

Example:

> Your main type is 3 / Ambitious Task Master. This suggests goal focus and adaptive execution are among your most available patterns.

### Trifix

Pattern:

> Your trifix is [code]. This means [main pattern] leads, supported by [second pattern] and [third pattern].

Example:

> Your trifix is 3-8-5. Achievement leads, supported by decisive protection and analytical control.

### Instinct Stack

Pattern:

> Your instinct stack is [stack]. This gives your learning examples a [primary instinct] emphasis.

Example:

> Your instinct stack is sp/so/sx. This gives your learning examples a practical security and sustainability emphasis.

### Course Recommendation

Pattern:

> Recommended because [result signal]. This course helps you [behavioral outcome].

Example:

> Recommended because Tough Protector is currently underused in your profile. This course helps you make cleaner requests and hold boundaries under pressure.

### Team Insight

Pattern:

> This team appears strong in [capacity]. The growth edge is [coordination challenge].

Example:

> This team appears strong in analysis and risk awareness. The growth edge is choosing action thresholds before planning becomes delay.

## Tone

Use language that is:

- Direct.
- Developmental.
- Specific.
- Non-clinical.
- Practical.

Avoid language that is:

- Mystical as the main explanation.
- Overly therapeutic.
- Overconfident.
- Cute or gimmicky.
- Shame-based.

## Disclaimer Copy

Short version:

> These results are for reflection and development. They are not clinical, diagnostic, or fixed.

Longer version:

> This profile is a development tool. It uses Enneagram-based patterns to suggest reflection prompts, skill courses, and team practices. It should not be treated as a clinical assessment or a fixed definition of personality.

## Open Copy Decisions

- Whether to use "Sexual" or "One-to-one" in the UI for `sx`.
- Whether "trifix" should be explained on first view or only in a tooltip.
- Whether the type names should be title case everywhere or sentence case in prose.

Current recommendation:

- Use "One-to-one" in primary UI with `sx` as the compact code.
- Explain trifix once in a short expandable note.
- Use title case for labels and sentence case in paragraphs.
