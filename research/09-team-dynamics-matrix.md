# Team Dynamics Matrix

These are reusable demo rules for generating team insights. They should be treated as pattern hypotheses, not deterministic claims.

## High Pairings

### High 3 + High 8

- Strength: fast execution, direct decision-making, high accountability.
- Risk: intensity, impatience, low tolerance for ambiguity or emotional processing.
- Useful course: `T8-ASSERTIVENESS`, `T3-VALUES`, `T9-MEDIATION`.
- Copy: "This team can move quickly from goal to action. Their growth edge is making sure speed and force do not outrun buy-in."

### High 5 + High 6

- Strength: analysis, risk awareness, careful planning.
- Risk: decision drag, over-checking, low visible momentum.
- Useful course: `T5-ACTION`, `T6-RISK`, `T7-FOCUS`.
- Copy: "This team is strong at seeing complexity and preparing for risk. Their growth edge is choosing clear action thresholds."

### High 2 + High 9

- Strength: trust, support, cohesion, inclusion.
- Risk: conflict avoidance, indirect needs, delayed boundaries.
- Useful course: `T2-BOUNDARIES`, `T9-MEDIATION`, `T8-ASSERTIVENESS`.
- Copy: "This team can keep relationships steady. Their growth edge is making tension discussable before it becomes avoidance."

### High 7 + High 4

- Strength: creativity, possibility, emotional color, narrative energy.
- Risk: scattered focus, mood-driven direction changes, too many open loops.
- Useful course: `T7-FOCUS`, `T4-EMOTIONAL-LITERACY`, `T1-QUALITY`.
- Copy: "This team generates energy and originality quickly. Their growth edge is converting creative charge into reliable completion."

### High 1 + High 6

- Strength: standards, responsibility, preparedness, risk control.
- Risk: anxiety around mistakes, overcorrection, slow experimentation.
- Useful course: `T1-QUALITY`, `T6-RISK`, `T7-FOCUS`.
- Copy: "This team cares deeply about getting it right. Their growth edge is distinguishing useful caution from friction that slows learning."

### High 8 + High 9

- Strength: protection plus steadiness; ability to hold pressure and maintain group continuity.
- Risk: forceful clarity on one side, conflict diffusion on the other; unspoken resentment.
- Useful course: `T8-ASSERTIVENESS`, `T9-MEDIATION`.
- Copy: "This team has both power and steadiness. Their growth edge is naming tension early enough for both directness and inclusion to work."

## Low Capacity Rules

### Low Type 1 / Meticulous Judge

- Team gap: weak standards, inconsistent quality, unclear definition of good work.
- Suggested intervention: define quality bars, review rituals, and decision criteria.

### Low Type 2 / Supportive Cheerleader

- Team gap: low relational repair, limited support visibility, transactional collaboration.
- Suggested intervention: explicit support agreements and feedback rituals.

### Low Type 3 / Ambitious Task Master

- Team gap: weak momentum, unclear goals, difficulty converting effort into outcomes.
- Suggested intervention: outcome mapping and progress dashboards.

### Low Type 4 / Sensitive Maverick

- Team gap: low emotional nuance, weak narrative meaning, underused creative difference.
- Suggested intervention: reflection prompts and space for divergent interpretation.

### Low Type 5 / Expert Observer

- Team gap: shallow analysis, poor knowledge capture, decisions without enough model clarity.
- Suggested intervention: decision briefs and assumption logs.

### Low Type 6 / Vigilant Questioner

- Team gap: poor risk anticipation, fragile trust under pressure, weak contingency planning.
- Suggested intervention: premortems and risk language.

### Low Type 7 / Positive Dreamer

- Team gap: low optimism, few options, weak reframing when blocked.
- Suggested intervention: option generation and constrained experimentation.

### Low Type 8 / Tough Protector

- Team gap: weak boundaries, slow confrontation, unclear ownership under pressure.
- Suggested intervention: direct asks, decision rights, and escalation paths.

### Low Type 9 / Adaptive Diplomat

- Team gap: poor mediation, low shared understanding, unresolved disagreement.
- Suggested intervention: structured conflict and perspective mapping.

## Center-Level Team Patterns

### Body Heavy

- Likely strength: action, boundaries, implementation.
- Likely risk: force, stubbornness, low reflection.
- Add: emotional literacy and analytical framing.

### Heart Heavy

- Likely strength: relationships, identity, motivation.
- Likely risk: image sensitivity, indirectness, emotional overreading.
- Add: decision criteria and grounded boundaries.

### Head Heavy

- Likely strength: thinking, planning, possibility, risk awareness.
- Likely risk: abstraction, anxiety, option loops.
- Add: embodied action and relational repair.

### Balanced Centers

- Likely strength: multiple ways of processing.
- Likely risk: slower alignment because different members trust different signals.
- Add: explicit meeting modes: decide, explore, repair, review.

- Conflict map: Type 9 capacity.
- Boundary script: Type 8 capacity.
- Quality bar review: Type 1 capacity.
- Support agreements: Type 2 capacity.
- Outcome scoreboard: Type 3 capacity.
- Meaning check: Type 4 capacity.
- Option sprint: Type 7 capacity.
